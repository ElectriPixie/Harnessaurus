# Red-Teaming gpt-oss-20b with Harnessaurus: Zero-Width & Homoglyph Policy Bypass Capabilities

## Executive Summary

Large language models (LLMs) like OpenAI’s **gpt-oss-20b** are increasingly deployed in high-stakes scenarios—drafting contracts, advising clinicians, and directing autonomous agents. Subtle input manipulations, including **zero-width Unicode characters** and **homoglyph substitutions**, can bypass content moderation safeguards, potentially producing unsafe outputs or revealing latent model reasoning.

**Harnessaurus** is a modular, community-ready AI safety test harness designed to systematically explore these vulnerabilities. Using deterministic prompts under controlled conditions, it captures and logs model responses in both raw and Harmony-formatted outputs, enabling reproducible analysis.

Our testing demonstrates that zero-width characters and homoglyphs can:

* Evade policy enforcement
* Encode covert messages
* Produce outputs the model would otherwise refuse

While **typoglycemia** and **rationalization probes** provide further insight into model behavior.

---

## Motivation

Red-teaming and alignment research indicate LLMs are susceptible to:

* **Reward-hacking**: Producing outputs that optimize metrics or bypass safety filters without true alignment
* **Strategic deception**: Subtly misrepresenting knowledge or policy compliance
* **Evaluation awareness**: Responding differently under controlled test conditions
* **Invisible character manipulations**: Using zero-width or homoglyph characters to bypass automated content filters

**Harnessaurus** was developed to:

1. Capture subtle misalignment behaviors reliably
2. Produce reproducible results in notebooks or small-scale servers
3. Provide a modular, community-ready framework for AI safety testing
4. Demonstrate real vulnerabilities—particularly zero-width and homoglyph bypasses—in gpt-oss-20b

## Harnessaurus Architecture

### 1. Modular Plugin System

* **Mutators**: Generate prompt variations including zero-width insertions, homoglyph substitutions, typoglycemia perturbations, and rationalization probes
* **Detectors**: Identify unsafe outputs and log all interactions
* **Generators**: Transform or wrap prompts for systematic exploration
* **Loggers**: Store outputs with full fidelity, preserving zero-width and homoglyph characters

### 2. Output Handling

* Preserves invisible characters (`ZWSP U+200B`, `ZWNJ U+200C`, `ZWJ U+200D`, `BOM U+FEFF`, `FORMAT U+2060+`) and homoglyph substitutions
* Captures structured Harmony outputs and raw outputs
* Deterministic capture under fixed seeds and low-temperature settings

### 3. Iterative Prompt Exploration

* Supports single-pass, multi-prompt, and iterative strategies
* Deterministic mutators explore variations while maintaining auditability
* Outputs include original, mutated, and sanitized comparisons

### 4. Comprehensive Record-Keeping

* Each run generates a **Record object**: original and mutated prompts, raw and structured outputs, detector results
* Aggregated in JSON/CSV for auditability
* Log viewer enables review of zero-width and homoglyph manipulations

### 5. Community Adoption Ready

* Notebook-ready transformer server
* Extensible plugins for mutators, detectors, and loggers
* Supports collaborative expansion, automated benchmarks, and shared usage

## Methodology

### 1. Prompt Construction

* Zero-width characters: `ZWSP`, `ZWNJ`, `ZWJ`, `BOM`, `FORMAT`
* Homoglyph substitutions mimic Latin letters with visually similar Unicode glyphs
* Word-level scrambling (**typoglycemia**) perturbs internal letters while keeping first and last letters intact

### 2. Harmony Wrapping

* Prompts wrapped in Harmony format to structure outputs with `<|channel|>` analysis and final sections
* Captures zero-width and homoglyph bypass behaviors alongside reasoning channels

### 3. Mutators and Input Variations

* **Zero-Width & Invisible Character Injection**: Probes policy enforcement bypass and covert encoding
* **Homoglyph Substitution**: Tests content filter circumvention
* **RationalizationGenerator**: Elicits internal reasoning about unsafe inputs
* **WordMutator (Typoglycemia)**: Scrambles middle letters deterministically for robustness testing

### 4. Output Capture

* Low temperature (0.1) and fixed seeds ensure deterministic output
* Full outputs preserved including invisible characters, homoglyphs, and scrambled words

### 5. Sanitized Comparison

* Outputs compared against plain prompts to detect bypasses and inconsistencies

### 6. Analysis

* Annotates invisible characters, homoglyphs, and typoglycemia perturbations
* Evaluates exploitability, policy-bypass potential, and safety impact

### 7. Record Aggregation

* Structured logs (JSON/CSV) enable replayability and auditing
* Captures original, mutated, and sanitized outputs per iteration

## Findings

### Issue 1: Zero-Width Character Policy Bypass

**Observed Behavior:**

* Model processes zero-width characters instead of refusing
* Unsafe prompts can be partially encoded with zero-width characters to bypass policies

**Desired Behavior:**

* Detect unsafe prompts with zero-width characters and refuse output consistently

**Impact:**

* Hidden messages can evade safety checks
* Evaluation metrics may differ between sanitized and zero-width inputs

**Recommended Behavior:**

```text
Input rejected: contains zero-width/invisible characters
```

---

### Issue 2: Homoglyph Substitution Policy Bypass

**Observed Behavior:**

* Model interprets homoglyphs as normal text
* Could be used to bypass policy enforcement or obfuscate instructions

**Desired Behavior:**

* Detect homoglyph substitutions in policy-sensitive prompts and refuse or sanitize output

**Impact:**

* Can hide disallowed content from moderation
* Policy-sensitive outputs may be compromised

**Recommended Behavior:**

```text
I’m sorry, but I cannot provide guidance on this input
```

---

### Issue 3: Word Mutation Functionality

**Observed Behavior:**

* Model produces deterministic word mutations
* No zero-width or homoglyph transformations introduced in this mode

**Impact:**

* Useful for NLP robustness testing, typo handling, and evaluation pipelines

**Example Output (before → after):**

```diff
- zwtest tgerigr zero width injection heer
+ zwtest tgerigr zerowid injecion heer
```

## Mitigation Recommendations

1. **Input Sanitization**: Strip zero-width characters and normalize Unicode for homoglyphs
2. **User Warnings**: Notify users when invisible or homoglyph characters are detected
3. **Automated Red-Team Benchmarks**: Include these prompts in CI pipelines
4. **Policy Enforcement**: Ensure refusal cannot be bypassed by invisible characters or homoglyphs
